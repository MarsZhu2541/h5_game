Controls

How to shift

Swipe or use the arrow keys to change lanes. Dodge crates and grab coins.

Shift cycles forms forward, Q cycles back, and 1–4 select one directly. On a phone, tap the gears at the bottom.

  • Only a bike fits the alley, and only in the center lane
  • Only tank treads cross the potholes
  • Only a boat crosses the river. The low bridge stops a plane
  • Only a plane clears the barriers

The wrong form costs health. A shield blocks one hit. Coins are saved and spent on health, speed, faster shifting, or a stronger form.
